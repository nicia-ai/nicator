## Reading

- If `proximity-260` / `proximity-520` / `proximity-1040` show the comparative gap shrinking monotonically toward the `judge` row, the regex's proximity window is the principal driver of the artifact.
- If `no-proximity` shows the gap close to the `judge` row, the issue is contiguity-anchoring specifically, not proximity per se.
- If `substring-canonical` / `bag-of-tokens` show large gaps relative to `judge`, surface-form sensitivity to canonical phrasing is the bigger driver than proximity.
- If the gap is roughly stable across all regex variants and only `judge` closes it, the strong-form 'surface-form scoring family is structurally undercounting' claim survives.
